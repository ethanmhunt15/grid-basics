Three experiments

  1. Find the cliff. Start at the base auction and raise the target one GW at a time. Note when a new supply block becomes marginal and reprices all cleared capacity.
  2. Retire cheap supply. Set existing coal to zero without changing demand. Compare the lost quantity with the change in gross annual payments.
  3. Separate nameplate from UCAP. Lower the hypothetical resource's accreditation while holding annual energy fixed. Which revenue stream changes?
